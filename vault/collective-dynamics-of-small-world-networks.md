The collective dynamics of small-world networks refers to a research area that investigates the properties and behavior of these complex systems. The work, first published in 1998 by Duncan Watts and Steven Strogatz, explores how small-world networks exhibit both local and global connectivity, leading to unique characteristics such as rapid information spread and robustness.
