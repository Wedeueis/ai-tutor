# Quantum Computers
Quantum computers are a new and exciting development in the field of computing. Unlike classical computers, which use bits (0s and 1s) to store and process information, quantum computers use qubits (quantum bits). Qubits can exist in multiple states at once, allowing them to process vast amounts of data exponentially faster than classical computers.
