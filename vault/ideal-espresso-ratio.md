title: Ideal Espresso Ratio
description: A suggested ratio for brewing high-quality espresso.
tags:

---

A good starting point for espresso is a 1:2 ratio of dose to yield, meaning that 18g of coffee should produce 36g of espresso over about 25-30 seconds. If the espresso runs faster than this, the grind may be too coarse; if it's slower, the grind is likely too fine.


For a clean and balanced cup, it's essential to maintain an ideal coffee-to-water ratio. A 1:16 ratio by weight, meaning one part coffee to sixteen parts water, is widely regarded as the sweet spot for pour-over brewing.
