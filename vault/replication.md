Replication is a crucial aspect of scientific inquiry. By repeating an experiment or study, researchers can verify the original results and increase confidence in their validity. Replication helps build upon existing knowledge and ensures that conclusions are based on robust evidence.


In academic papers, it's crucial to disclose when generative AI tools were used for tasks such as data analysis or rephrasing sections. This disclosure maintains the integrity and credibility of the research.
